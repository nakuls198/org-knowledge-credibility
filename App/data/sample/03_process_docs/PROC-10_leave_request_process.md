# PROC-10 — Leave Request Process

- **Owner:** Jamie Lee, Operations Manager
- **Last updated:** 2024-06-01
- **Related policy:** POL-10

## Steps

1. Employee submits a leave request in the HR system.
2. Requests longer than 5 days are routed to the Head of People for approval.
3. Approved leave is recorded in the team calendar.

## Exceptions and escalation path

Not documented.

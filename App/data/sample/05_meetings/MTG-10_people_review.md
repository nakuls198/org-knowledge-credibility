# MTG-10 — People Review

- **Date:** 2024-12-10
- **Participants:** Morgan Blake (CEO), Priyanka Rao (Head of People), Jamie Lee (Operations Manager)

## Discussion

Unplanned absences were disrupting rosters. Priyanka proposed that every leave request should be approved by the employee's manager.

## Options considered

1. Keep HR approval above 5 days only.
2. Require manager approval for all leave.

## Final decision

Require manager approval for all leave requests from 2025-01-01, with Head of People approval for leave over 10 days.

## Actions

- Priyanka Rao to publish the updated leave policy POL-10. (Done — see POL-10.)
- No one was assigned to update PROC-10.
